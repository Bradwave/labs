orbitxr

Launch a ship from a tower, then fly it from the cockpit (VR) or watch the orbit from outside on your table (AR). Same physics as Orbit Lab.

drag: look around · wheel: zoom · W S D A: thrusters · Space: play · R: reset · V: view

How it works

Free fall around a planet. The ship starts at the top of a tower. Throw it sideways: gravity keeps pulling it toward the centre, and at one speed the ground curves away as fast as the ship falls. That is an orbit.

Outside view (AR / screen)

The planet floats in front of you. Pinch or grip near it to move, turn and (with two hands) scale it. Arrows: velocity (green), gravity (blue), thrust (magenta). The dashed curve is the predicted orbit.

Ship view (VR)

You sit in the cockpit: up is away from the planet, forward is the direction of motion. Green circle = prograde, red cross = retrograde. Objects in the cabin float while you coast and drift backward when you burn.

Controls in the headset

Trigger or hand-ray pinch presses panel buttons (hold a thruster to burn, tap for a small Δv step). Controllers: A/B burn prograde/retrograde, X/Y radial out/in, stick press hides the panel. Fingertip poke works too.
