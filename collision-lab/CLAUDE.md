# Collision Lab

Follow `../DESIGN.md`: it is the design standard of all the labs (header, settings menu, components, Italian and English, plots, PWA, repository layout). This file only adds what is specific to Collision Lab.

- What it is: Collisions in two dimensions on an air table: momentum, centre of mass and the energy that is lost.
- Run locally: `python3 -m http.server 8100` in the repository root, then open http://localhost:8100/collision-lab/ (the page loads the shared `../kit`).
- The shared kit is `../kit/`. `sw.js` and `manifest.webmanifest` are generated by `node ../tools/sync-kit.mjs collision-lab`: never edit them by hand.
- Before a release: `node ../tools/sync-kit.mjs collision-lab` then `node ../tools/check-app.mjs collision-lab`.
- Strings: `js/strings.js`, same keys in `it` and `en`. Interface default is Italian.
- Tests: `node --test tests/`.

## Notes
- Not Carts Lab (one rail experiment with sensors and data analysis) and not Impulse Lab (the force during one collision): this lab is about conservation, in 2D, in the lab and centre-of-mass frames.
- Physics is pure and tested: `js/physics.js` (discs, rigid clusters, exact contact times, sticking, explosions, bumpers) and `js/scenes.js` (experiments, drag handles). Model: `docs/MODEL.md`.
- Disc colours: `--d1…--d5` (fills, both themes) and `--l1…--l5` (lines in graphs and diagrams, darker in the light theme); physics quantities use the kit tokens (`--vel`, `--mom`, `--ek`).
- Dragging works only at t = 0 and in the lab frame. Settings travel in the URL hash (`js/state.js`); display switches are stored as `collision-lab.show`.
- Version: `VERSION` in `js/main.js` and `package.json`.
